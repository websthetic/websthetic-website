Frequently Asked Questions.

Have a question about working with us? We’re happy to talk it through.

Ask Us Anything
Can you work completely white-label?

Yes. We can remain entirely behind the scenes while your agency owns the client relationship and presents the work under your brand.

Can we just send you our Figma files?

Yes. Approved designs, content, assets, requirements, and functionality are typically enough for us to begin a technical review and scope the development.

Do you communicate directly with our clients?

Only if you want us to. We can communicate exclusively through your team or join client conversations when technical input would be useful.

Can we use Websthetic only when we’re busy?

Absolutely. Some agencies work with us project by project, while others use us as an ongoing development partner.

What happens if the client changes the design during development?

Small implementation adjustments are part of the process. Larger changes to design, functionality, or scope may require an updated timeline and cost before continuing.

Do you build with accessibility in mind?

Yes. Accessibility considerations are incorporated throughout our development process. If a client requires a specific WCAG target or formal accessibility audit, we can identify that during scoping so the appropriate level of work is planned.

Can you maintain the site after launch?

Yes. We can provide ongoing development, maintenance, updates, and technical support after the project launches.

How do you price agency projects?

Projects are typically scoped based on design complexity, functionality, integrations, CMS requirements, timeline, and development effort. Once we review the project, we provide a clear scope and cost before development begins.
