Frequently Asked Questions.

Still have a question about your project? We’re happy to talk it through.

Ask Us Anything
How much does a website cost?

Every project is different depending on size, functionality, content, and complexity. After learning about your project, we’ll provide a clear scope and price before work begins.

How long does a website take?

Most projects follow a defined timeline based on the size and complexity of the build. We’ll establish your timeline before the project begins.

Do I need to provide all the content?

We’ll let you know exactly what we need and help organize the content required for the site.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can rebuild an existing website while keeping what works and improving what doesn’t.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Every website we build is designed and tested for desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Do you build accessible websites?

Accessibility is considered throughout our design and development process. If your organization requires a specific accessibility standard or formal compliance target, let us know during discovery so it can be properly scoped.

Can I update the website myself?

Depending on the project, we can build editable content areas so your team can manage common updates.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Websthetic can continue supporting your website through ongoing maintenance, updates, and development.
