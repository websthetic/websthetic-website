Accessibility Statement.

We want everyone to be able to use our website, whatever device, browser, or assistive technology they use.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Our Commitment

Accessibility is part of how we design and build every website, including our own. We aim for this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 (opens in a new tab) at Level AA, in line with the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA).

What We’ve Done

  • Readable colour contrast. Text meets WCAG AA contrast ratios. The Change Colour feature only uses colour combinations that keep text readable.
  • Keyboard friendly. Links, buttons, menus, and form fields can be used with a keyboard and show a visible focus style.
  • Screen reader support. Pages use clear headings and landmarks, images have text alternatives, and interactive tools announce changes.
  • Labelled forms. Every form field has a visible label, and required fields are clearly marked.
  • Reduced motion. If you’ve turned on “reduce motion” in your device settings, animations are switched off or kept to a minimum.
  • Responsive layouts. The site adapts to any screen size and supports zooming in up to 200%.

Known Limitations

We’re always working to improve, and some areas may not be fully accessible yet:

  • Websites in our portfolio belong to our clients and are outside of our control.
  • The colour previews in our design tools are visual by nature. We include the colour codes and contrast results as text alongside them.

Tell Us About A Barrier

If anything on this website is difficult to use, or you need information in a different format, please let us know. Tell us the page and what happened, and we’ll do our best to respond within two business days.
