About Colour Harmonies

Colour harmonies are combinations based on where colours sit on the colour wheel. They’re a reliable starting point for palettes that feel balanced.

  • Complementary: colours from opposite sides of the wheel, for strong contrast and energy.
  • Monochromatic: light and dark shades of one colour, calm and cohesive.
  • Analogous: neighbouring colours on the wheel, natural and easy on the eye.
  • Triadic: three colours evenly spaced around the wheel, vibrant but balanced.
  • Random: anything goes, great for finding something unexpected.

Planning to use these colours for text? Check them with our Colour Contrast Checker so they stay readable for everyone.
